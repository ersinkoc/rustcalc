# Code of Conduct

RustCalc welcomes contributors regardless of background, experience, identity
or location. Treat people respectfully and discuss technical disagreements with
evidence and constructive language.

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discrimination, unwanted sexual attention,
doxxing and deliberate disruption are unacceptable. Do not publish another
person's private information without permission.

This applies to all project spaces. Maintainers may remove harmful content,
close discussions or restrict participation, proportionately and with community
safety in mind. Report concerns privately to [Ersin Koç](https://github.com/ersinkoc)
using an available private contact method. Do not post sensitive details publicly.
This volunteer project does not guarantee a response time.
